# Product & Solution Portfolio

PT Taran Rainmaker Group develops digital products and technology solutions for healthcare, document workflows, and organizational operations.

This page is a high-level portfolio overview. Product scope, implementation status, integrations, and availability are confirmed individually during a product discussion or demonstration. A product name or description on this page must not be interpreted as a certification, regulatory approval, or claim of deployment at a customer facility.

## Healthcare technology

### TaranPx
Hospital and clinic information system (SIMRS) and electronic medical record (RME), including a digital Nurse Calling capability.

- Product category: Healthcare information system
- Product information: [TaranPx repository](https://github.com/taranrainmaker/taranpx)
- Product access and demonstration: https://px.taranrainmaker.my.id

### TaranDx
Solution for INA-CBG and iDRG healthcare claim analysis and monitoring.

### TaranDispute
Solution focused on healthcare claim dispute analysis and supporting workflows.

### TaranMidwife
A digital platform concept for midwifery and home-care service workflows, including patient and partner-facing mobile application development.

- Project repository: [TaranMidwife](https://github.com/taranrainmaker/taranmidwife)
- The project is under development; feature maturity and release status are described in its repository documentation.

### TaranHx
A product concept for healthcare operations, quality and accreditation workflows, administration, human resources, and reporting.
