## 3. Approval boundaries

Approval of policy documents does not, by itself:
- Prove that a technical control is configured or operating effectively.
- Replace evidence required by a security assessment.
- Constitute a penetration test, independent audit, certification, or regulator approval.
- Change the score of a SATUSEHAT assessment workbook.
- Appoint a named PIC where the appointee field remains blank.
- Execute an NDA or customer SLA on behalf of another party.

The Director or authorized company officer should sign the controlled master copies. Signed copies should be stored in a restricted company repository with version history and access controls.
